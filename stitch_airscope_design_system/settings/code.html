Route Sub-Index (Route_Index_i)

Route_Index_{i,t} = \left( \frac{\tilde{P}_{i,t}}{\tilde{P}_{i,0}} \right) \times 100

Where P̃ is the median canonical airfare across tranches.
Composite National Airfare Index (AIRSCOPE APIx)

APIx_t = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{M} \left[ w_i \times \text{Route\_Index}_{i,t} \right]}{\sum_{i=1}^{M} w_i}

Where w_i is the sovereign DGCA O-D seat volume weight.
