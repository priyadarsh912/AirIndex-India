## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, production-grade intelligence environment engineered for civil aviation analysts, national statistical authorities, and enterprise airfare revenue teams. The visual direction balances the crisp utilitarian efficiency of government statistical dashboards with the clean, elevated ergonomics of modern enterprise SaaS. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Corporate & Modern Precision:** White and pale blue-gray foundational surfaces create a serene, low-glare canvas designed for continuous monitoring and micro-data extraction.
- **Empirical Credibility:** Structural clarity is achieved via precise 1px hairline rules and restrained surface shifts, foregoing heavy shadows or skeuomorphic textures in favor of clean information layering.
- **Dynamic Aviation Accents:** A dominant sovereign navy anchor complemented by vivid cobalt signals, precision teal indicators, and decisive categorical telemetry colors (positive green, volatility amber, and alert red).
- **Tabular Rigor:** Structured alignment with deliberate visual pacing, facilitating instantaneous scanning of tabular metrics, route corridors, lead-time indices, and high-frequency currency (₹ INR) rates.

## Colors

The color palette provides immediate semantic categorization while preserving an authoritative, institutional appearance.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#123B7A`):** The primary brand anchor. Applied to top-level navigation states, key primary buttons, modal headers, and primary branding elements. Imparts executive weight and governance.
- **Secondary Electric Blue (`#2F6FED`):** The interactive engine. Utilized for active toggle segments, primary chart line paths, interactive link states, and prominent callouts.
- **Tertiary Deep Teal (`#17A6A1`):** Represents operational stability and data collection telemetry, including coverage rings, sync metrics, and secondary analytical points.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F1F5F9`):** `#F8FAFC` forms the global viewport background, while pristine `#FFFFFF` elevates individual cards and analytical clusters. `#F1F5F9` serves as the neutral fill for segmented controls, table headers, and pill backings.
- **Hairline Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Single-pixel perimeter rule deployed uniformly across cards, dividers, input borders, and tabular gridlines.

### Status & Telemetry Scale
- **Positive / Stable (`#16A34A`):** Denotes downward fare trends, active scraper health, and index stabilization. Paired with soft tint `#DCFCE7`.
- **Warning / Surge (`#EA580C`):** Flags moderate route inflation, volatile pricing spikes, and attention-requiring pipelines. Paired with `#FFEDD5`.
- **Critical / Sharp Increase (`#DC2626`):** Identifies anomalous high fare surges, API outages, or pipeline errors. Paired with `#FEE2E2`.

## Typography

The typography couples the architectural balance of IBM Plex Sans with the optical clarity of Inter.

### Functional Roles
- **IBM Plex Sans (Headlines & Quantitative Metrics):** Powers all dashboard section titles, summary hero displays, and high-impact numerical indicators. Numerals must be configured with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time data refreshes.
- **Inter (Body, Tables, Controls & Microcopy):** Drives high-density data tables, route matrices, dropdown selectors, badges, and contextual annotations. Its neutral grotesque geometry allows dense information scanning without visual fatigue.
- **Currency & Figures Rule:** Indian Rupee symbols (₹) and quantitative percentage shifts always match the font weight and tabular baseline of their accompanying numeric string.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an enterprise fluid grid that adapts dynamically across resolutions while preserving maximum data density.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Layout Model:** A structured sidebar shell (fixed 240px width on desktop) paired with a responsive 12-column main canvas. 
- **Column Gaps & Margins:** Grid columns maintain a uniform 16px (`1rem`) gutter. Section margins default to 24px (`1.5rem`) on desktop and scale to 16px (`1rem`) on compact displays.
- **Card Padding Cadence:** Metric summary tiles use `space-lg` (16px) internal padding, while complex analytic panes (charts, route tables) utilize `space-xl` (24px) padding to define clear visual chapters.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop Wide (>= 1440px)`: Full 3-column analysis view (primary analytics 8-columns, telemetry/insights 4-columns).
  - `Desktop Regular (1024px - 1439px)`: 2-column stacked flow with persistent sidebar.
  - `Tablet & Mobile (< 1024px)`: Sidebar collapses to a drawer or bottom tab strip. Metric clusters collapse from 4-across to a 2x2 grid. Data tables adopt smooth horizontal scroll containers with pinned route identity columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface separation and ultra-soft, low-opacity ambient shadows rather than heavy drop-shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` background. Completely matte, providing a non-distracting baseline for overlaid analysis cards.
- **Level 1 (Analytical Cards & Tables):** Elevated via pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and an ambient diffused shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** On hover or selection, elevation softly enhances to `0px 4px 8px rgba(18, 59, 122, 0.06), 0px 8px 24px rgba(18, 59, 122, 0.04)`, subtly shifting the border hue to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Popovers, Select Menus, Tooltips):** Overlaid floating interfaces utilize `0px 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10)` bounded by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` edge. Backdrops for full modals employ an ultra-subtle translucent wash (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)`) with minimal 2px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a calibrated **8px (`0.5rem`)** corner radius across cards, form inputs, metric blocks, and primary containers. This provides a balance between modern visual polish and the rigorous structure demanded by high-volume data software.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Base Containers & Cards (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to all dashboard panels, analytical cards, hero banners, and dialog windows.
- **Interactive Controls (`0.375rem` / 6px to `0.5rem` / 8px):** Search inputs, action buttons, table filter pills, and dropdown selectors align to the 6px–8px radius.
- **Indicators & Micro-Badges (Full Pill / 9999px):** Status badges (e.g., "+3.2%", "Live Data", "Online"), avatar backdrops, and category chips utilize full rounded pill caps to differentiate categorical tags from structural interactive surfaces.
- **Progress & Sparkline Tracks:** Circular indicator rings and sparkline endpoints utilize soft 2px corner joins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Primary Navy (`#123B7A`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px radius, height 40px, font weight 600. Hover state transitions to `#1B4E9B`.
- **Accent / Interactive:** Electric Blue (`#2F6FED`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for immediate data interactions (e.g., "Download Report", range updates).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Icon Utility:** 36px x 36px square with 8px radius, subtle border, housing notification bells, profile icons, and quick-filter switches.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Search Bar:** Height 42px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded 8px. Text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state applies `border-color: #2F6FED` and an ambient glow of `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(47, 111, 237, 0.12)`.
- **Filter Dropdowns:** Compact 32px height, subtle arrow indicator, text Inter 12px Medium, background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.

### Data Cards & Metric Displays
- **Metric Cards:** White `#FFFFFF` card, 8px radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, 16px internal padding. Label in Inter 12px `#64748B`, metric figure in IBM Plex Sans 28px `#0F172A` with tabular numeral spacing. 
- **Inline Trend Badges:** Pill-shaped pill chips displaying variance (e.g., `▲ 3.2%`, `▼ 1.2%`). Green text on `#DCFCE7` for decreases/positive health; Red text on `#FEE2E2` for rapid price escalations.
- **Lead-Time Bar Strips:** Minimal categorical bar charts with muted `#F1F5F9` background columns and dynamic `#2F6FED` active fare bars, topped with tabular value callouts.

### Data Tables & Route Ledgers
- **Header:** Height 36px, background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Inter 11px font weight 600, uppercase text-transform with subtle letter spacing, color `#64748B`.
- **Rows:** Minimum height 44px, alternating backgrounds disabled, border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover row activates `#F8FAFC`.
- **Data Alignment:** Route codes (e.g., `DEL → BOM`) and airline identifiers left-aligned; lead-time numbers, base fares (₹), and variance percentages strictly right-aligned with fixed tabular figures.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- **Live Ingestion Telemetry:** 8px glowing status dot (Green `#16A34A` for live sync, Amber `#EA580C` for ingest delays, Red `#DC2626` for connector drops) paired with Inter 11px Medium status text.
- **Segmented Range Selectors:** Pill container in `#F1F5F9`, holding interactive segment buttons (`7D`, `30D`, `90D`, `1Y`). Active selection styled in Primary Navy (`#123B7A`) with white text, while inactive options remain unbordered `#64748B`.
