science Methodology & Empirical Standard

Modified Laspeyres Chain-Weighted Price Formulation

The sovereign Airfare Price Index (APIx) is computed using daily high-frequency fare observations across 124 trunk and regional pairs, normalising for seat availability, ancillary unbundling, and departure lead time windows. Passenger weights ($w_i$) are recalibrated quarterly from official DGCA civil passenger load manifests. Outlier detection operates via non-linear Tukey trimean filters at the route-airline level to ensure unskewed empirical veracity for national statistical consumption and Consumer Price Index (CPI) transport basket augmentation.

Mathematical Formula Eq. 4.2
APIx_t = 100 × ∑ [ w_i × (P_{i,t} / P_{i,0}) ]
